What is an Image Resizer?

An image resizer is an online tool that allows you to change the width and height of an image. It can be useful when you need a specific image size for websites, applications, forms, social media or documents.

How to Resize an Image Online?

  1. Select your JPG, PNG or other image.
  2. Enter the required width and height.
  3. Keep "Keep aspect ratio" enabled if you want to maintain the original proportions.
  4. Select the output format.
  5. Click Resize Image.
  6. Download your resized image.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I resize JPG images online?

Yes. You can upload a JPG image, enter the required dimensions and download the resized image.

Can I resize PNG images?

Yes. PNG images can also be resized using this tool.

Will my image be uploaded to a server?

The resizing process is performed directly in your browser using JavaScript and the HTML Canvas API.

What does Keep Aspect Ratio mean?

Keeping the aspect ratio prevents the image from becoming stretched or distorted when you change its dimensions.
