About AB Digital Utility

Simple digital tools for everyday work.

What is AB Digital Utility?

AB Digital Utility is a free online utility platform designed to make common digital tasks easier and faster.

Our goal is to provide simple browser-based tools that people can use without installing complicated software.

What can you do here?

You can use tools such as JPG to PDF conversion, image compression, image resizing, QR code generation, GST calculation, percentage calculation, age calculation and EMI calculation.

Our Goal

We want AB Digital Utility to become a useful collection of everyday online tools that are simple, fast and accessible from computers, tablets and mobile devices.

Free to Use

Our tools are designed to be easy to access and use. We continuously work on improving existing tools and adding new utilities based on common user needs.

Privacy First

Many of our tools process files directly in your browser, so your files do not need to be uploaded to our server for those tools to work.

Thank you for using AB Digital Utility.
